Start cutscene (3 frames):
Time travel: Haven in a purple and cyan portal with floating clocks and 0s and 1s.
Gang hideout: a lamp-lit room where Haven (marked “!”) walks in on three gangsters at a card table.
Chased: a dusk street with the mob running after Haven, fists up.
Loop backgrounds: Market (striped stalls, MARKET sign on the roof), Alley (night brick wall, fire escape, WANTED poster) and Street (Bakery, Shoes and Tailor shops). Each one repeats seamlessly left to right, so you can scroll them forever.
Obstacles: carriage, trash can and gas-lamp pole, all on transparent backgrounds.
Home: an evening end scene with a lit-up brick house. There’s also the house on its own, so you can place it at the end of the level.
Death screen: a “CAUGHT!” poster panel and a RESTART button.
Loading screen: a background with the title, a pocket watch and “LOADING...”. The loading bar comes in two pieces, the empty frame and the fill. They’re the same size, so you stack one on top of the other.
Main menu:
a dusk street background and the title logo
PLAY, SETTINGS and CREDITS buttons
blank SETTINGS and CREDITS pages, plus a BACK button, a volume slider and a checkbox
every button has normal, hover and pressed versions

Size: the backgrounds are 384×216, so scaled up exactly 3× they fill Godot’s default 1152×648 window. To keep the pixels sharp instead of blurry, go to Project Settings → Rendering → Textures and set Default Texture Filter to Nearest. The README in the zip lists every file.
